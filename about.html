Who is MRP?

MRP (Msanzi Referendum Party) is intending to register as a political party that will transform SA political landscape into the two party state electoral system as is in the United States of America. It will be divided into two parts namely:

  • Division B

Division A

Is a political party that is made up of people that believes in life sentence for murder. In this party private ownership of guns is allowed provided the license is required.

Division B
